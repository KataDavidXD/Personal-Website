Welcome to my corner of the internet.

This is where I share my thoughts, projects, and learnings at the intersection of **artificial intelligence**, **game development**, and **philosophical inquiry**.

## What You'll Find Here

### 🤖 Agent Infrastructure

Deep dives into building robust multi-agent systems, LLM architectures, and the infrastructure that powers intelligent automation. From theoretical foundations to production-ready implementations.

### 🎮 Game Engine Development

Explorations in real-time rendering, physics simulation, and game engine architecture. Building interactive experiences from first principles.

### 💭 Thinking

Reflections on technology's role in society, decision-making frameworks, and the philosophical questions that arise when building intelligent systems.

## Why a Personal Website?

In an age of social media and algorithmic feeds, a personal website is a statement of ownership. It's a place where ideas can breathe, evolve, and be discovered on their own terms.

Here, I can:

- **Write long-form content** that wouldn't fit in a tweet thread
- **Update and refine** ideas as my understanding grows
- **Build a searchable archive** of my work
- **Own my content** without platform dependencies
