## Overview

This paper addresses the challenge of coordinating multiple autonomous agents in environments that change during task execution.

## Key Contributions

1. **Hierarchical Planning Framework**: A novel approach that decomposes complex tasks into manageable subtasks
2. **Adaptive Communication Protocol**: Efficient message passing that scales with agent count
3. **Benchmark Results**: State-of-the-art performance on three standard coordination benchmarks
